# Targeted full-paper evidence for the public-data-first topic search

Date: 2026-07-16

This note complements the earlier 170-paper corpus. Ten additional PDFs were downloaded, rendered, validated and extracted in full. Their text audit is in `tmp/pdfs/bego-literature/public-data-topics/pdf-audit.json`; first-page renders are in `tmp/pdfs/bego-literature/public-data-topics/rendered/`. A Scientific Reports paper was read through its open HTML because the publisher returned an HTML anti-bot page for the PDF URL.

The central audit rule is stricter than “the paper used real data”: the dataset used for the proposed thesis's core claim must be independently public, downloadable, sufficiently documented and compatible with the claimed outcome. A private dataset, a public dataset used only to calibrate synthetic requests, or real check-ins combined with synthetic groups does not satisfy that rule.

## Consequences for topic selection

1. **Reject BeGo-LTF as the primary thesis claim.** No public dataset in the 170-paper corpus or the targeted search contains repeated, identifiable real groups with successive outing choices, per-member burden, attendance/outcome and sufficient history to validate mobility-debt repayment. Synthetic histories can test invariants, not empirical superiority.
2. **Reject real-group preference or negotiation claims built from random grouping.** Public individual check-ins do not become a real group dataset by randomly batching users.
3. **Allow counterfactual operational replay.** For routing/dispatch, the public observations are requests, trajectories or labels. A solver may make counterfactual decisions on those observations as long as every method sees the same requests/network and the claimed outcome is an operational metric directly computed from the replay.
4. **Separate model layers.** A public perception dataset can prove curb-ramp detection; a public barrier-rating dataset can prove group-specific edge costs; and route replay can prove path exposure reductions. None alone proves all three.
5. **Use strict chronology and external validation.** Random row splits are prohibited for temporal mobility tasks. At least one held-out day/month/city and multiple random seeds are required.
