## Interpretation boundary

Reading a paper does not make its claims true for BeGo. Every transfer is recorded as a hypothesis, design constraint, baseline, or experiment. In particular, approximation guarantees are never transferred across objectives, simulated recommender satisfaction is not treated as realized mobility burden, and accessibility tags are not treated as ground truth without provenance and confidence.
